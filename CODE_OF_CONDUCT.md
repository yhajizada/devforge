# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in DevForge a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions and viewpoints
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the overall community
